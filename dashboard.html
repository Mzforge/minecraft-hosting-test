Download your server pack

One zip with everything configured. Unzip it, run the start file, and you're hosting.

What lands in the zip. server.properties from your settings, eula.txt already accepted, your plugins inside /plugins (or /mods), start.bat and start.sh, plus playit.exe when that box is ticked.

Use your own domain

Point play.yourdomain.com at your server so friends never type an IP.

First, get your tunnel address.
1
Run start.bat from your downloaded pack. It launches playit.exe in the background automatically.
2
First run only: the console prints a one-time claim link like https://playit.gg/claim/abc123. Open it in your browser, sign in free, and click Allow.
3
Your address appears at playit.gg/account/tunnels — something like logo-bacon.gl.joinmc.link. Paste it below.
The part before your domain — e.g. play gives play.yourdomain.com.
